Hello Friend

A friend who shows up when you need it most.
Let's get you set up.

1

Write your message

Click the 🙂 icon in your Chrome toolbar. Write something to your future self — something only you would say. This is the message that will appear when you need it.

2

Enable in incognito

Go to chrome://extensions → find Hello Friend → click Details → turn on Allow in Incognito. This is the most important step — your future self needs this.

3

You're protected

Hello Friend now runs silently in the background. When you need it, it will show up. No notifications, no tracking, no data collected. Just you and your message.
